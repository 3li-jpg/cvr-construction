# Current gallery policy (client update, 2026-10-15)

Only finished-project photos appear in project galleries, service galleries and the general gallery. Original-condition and construction-stage photos have been removed from those collections. Each project with a confirmed comparison keeps exactly one selected before image, exclusively in its transformation slider. Projects without a confirmed before remain without a slider. Source files are preserved.

This policy supersedes the publication decisions and gallery counts in the historical audit below. The source review and uncertain pairings remain valid.
